FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are diagnostics really free?

Yes. Diagnostics are free. I'll look at the device, identify the problem as best as possible, and explain what needs to be done before you decide whether to move forward with a paid repair.

What devices do you repair?

I currently work with phones, laptops, desktop PCs, game consoles, printers, tablets, and other everyday technology. Contact me if you're unsure whether I can help with your device.

How much does a repair cost?

Prices depend on the device, problem, and parts required. Check the Prices page for starting prices. You'll receive an estimate before paid repair work begins.

Do I need an appointment?

Please contact Weber's Tech Repair Shop before bringing in a device so we can discuss the problem and arrange a convenient time.

Do you provide the parts?

Parts can be ordered when needed. Parts are generally charged separately from the repair labor.
