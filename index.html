FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are diagnostics really free?

Yes. Diagnostics are free. I'll look at the device, identify the problem as best as possible, and explain what needs to be done before you decide whether to move forward with a paid repair.

What devices do you repair?

I work with phones, laptops, desktop PCs, game consoles, printers, tablets, and other everyday technology. Contact me if you're unsure whether I can help with your device.

How much does a repair cost?

Prices depend on the device, problem, and parts required. Check the Prices page for starting prices. You'll receive an estimate before paid repair work begins.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. Device drop-offs, pickups, and home visits are by approved appointment only. You can request an appointment using the Square booking link on this website.

Do you offer home visits?

Yes. Home visits are available upon request in Mattoon and surrounding towns. Availability and any applicable fees are confirmed before the appointment.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash is accepted. Secure online invoice payments may also be available by arrangement. Payment details are provided before any paid work begins.

Do you provide the parts?

Parts can be ordered when needed. Parts are generally charged separately from repair labor.
