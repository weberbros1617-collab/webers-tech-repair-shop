FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are diagnostics really free?

Yes. Diagnostics are free. I'll inspect your device and explain what needs to be done before you decide whether to move forward with a paid repair.

What devices do you repair?

I work with phones, laptops, desktop PCs, game consoles, printers, tablets, and other everyday technology.

How much does a repair cost?

Prices depend on the device, problem, and parts required. Check the Prices page for starting prices. You'll receive an estimate before paid repair work begins.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. Device drop-offs, pickups, and home visits are by approved appointment only. You can request an appointment through our Square booking page.

Why do I need to sign a repair waiver?

The waiver documents your authorization for repair services and explains important repair terms and risks. You'll receive a separate electronic waiver through Square Contracts before your appointment is approved.

Do you offer home visits?

Yes. Home visits are available upon request in Mattoon and surrounding communities. Availability and applicable fees will be confirmed before the appointment.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash is accepted. Secure online invoice payments may also be available by arrangement. Payment details are provided before any paid repair work begins.

Do you provide replacement parts?

Parts can be ordered when needed. Parts are generally charged separately from repair labor.

Where is your repair shop located?

Weber's Tech Repair Shop is a home-based business in Mattoon, Illinois. The exact drop-off address is provided privately after your appointment is approved.
